As a beginner, all you need is a comfortable, quiet space to sit for a few minutes each day. Wear loose clothing and optionally use a yoga mat or meditation cushion for spine support.
Guided meditation provides an instructor's voice leading your visualization and breath, whereas unguided meditation relies on silent personal focus.
We recommend starting with just 5 to 10 minutes daily. Consistency is far more valuable than session duration when building the habit.
Start with our foundational "Easy Fitness Setup" path, which introduces fundamental seated postures, deep diaphragmatic breathing, and gentle spinal warmups.
