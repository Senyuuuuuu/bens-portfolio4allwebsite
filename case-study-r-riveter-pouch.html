The Challenge

Highlighting Handcrafted Tactility: Staging Detailed Embroidery and Rugged Duck Canvas

The Embroidered Dandelion Canvas Pouch is one of R. Riveter's most intimate, tactile handcrafted pieces. Because the hero element is delicate floral needlework against textured natural brown duck canvas, standard catalog cutouts can struggle to communicate scale, hand-feel, and artisanal luxury.

The challenge was to create an editorial workspace scene—surrounding the pouch with authentic stationery, rolled leather shoulder strap with brass trigger snaps, ceramic pen holder, and crystal paperweights—while ensuring the pouch remained the unmistakable focal point bathed in natural window daylight.
