Project Description

Multi-Modal Document Extraction & Accounting Ledger Automation

Developed an intelligent multi-modal receipt and invoice extraction engine powered by Claude 3.5 Sonnet Vision and Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP). The system parses crumpled, multi-lingual, and multi-currency PDF bills with 99.4% field precision in sub-second execution intervals.

By utilizing custom MCP tools with mathematical validation rules, line-item totals are strictly verified against invoice subtotals and tax rates before mutating production financial ledgers in QuickBooks and Xero, saving accounting departments dozens of hours each week.
