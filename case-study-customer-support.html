The Bottleneck

Manual Inbound Triage Causing Severe Lead Drop-Off

Fast-growing agencies and tech companies receive hundreds of inbound inquiries across contact forms, live chat, and emails. By the time a sales rep manually reviewed an inquiry 4 hours later, the potential client had already contacted competitors.

Core Challenges
  • 4.2-hour average response delay losing warm sales opportunities.
  • Spam and student solicitations cluttering sales executive inboxes.
  • Incomplete CRM deal tags and missing meeting booking links.
Engineered Solutions
  • Autonomous n8n webhook receiver responding in under 20 seconds.
  • Claude 3.5 Sonnet zero-shot intent classifier scoring lead value (0-100).
  • Auto-generated Calendly invite links with automated VIP Slack pings.
Autonomous Pipeline

Multi-Agent n8n Decision Topology

We constructed a deterministic execution graph in n8n leveraging structured JSON outputs, Supabase vector embeddings for knowledge retrieval, and bidirectional CRM sync.

STAGE 01
Webhook Ingestion & Sanitization
Normalizes payloads across Typeform, Webflow, and Gmail into a standard JSON schema in <15ms.
STAGE 02
Claude 3.5 Sonnet Reasoning
Analyzes budget, timeframe, and technical requirements with chain-of-thought verification and scores intent.
STAGE 03
CRM & Database Sync
Creates or updates contact in Hubspot CRM with lead grade, calculated deal value, and personalized context notes.
STAGE 04
Slack VIP Notification & Auto-Reply
Dispatches instant email reply with customized discovery questions while sending interactive one-click approve cards to Slack.
