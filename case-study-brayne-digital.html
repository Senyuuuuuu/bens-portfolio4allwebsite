Project Description

Unified Print & Digital Collateral for a Modern Tech Consultancy

Brayne Digital required a cohesive corporate brand overhaul capable of seamlessly translating across physical marketing collateral and high-resolution digital touchpoints. We created a modern geometric visual language centered on clarity, professional confidence, and tech-forward sophistication.

The deliverables included factory-calibrated tri-fold brochures with exact bleed margins and die-lines, enamel coffee mug merchandise mockups, official business stationery, and social media marketing banners calibrated to TESDA Visual Graphic Design NC III professional standards.
