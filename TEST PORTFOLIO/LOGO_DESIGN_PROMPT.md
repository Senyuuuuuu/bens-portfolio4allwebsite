# BENYAMIN NAMTALASHVILI — BRAND LOGO SPECIFICATION & GENERATIVE PROMPTS

## 1. Brand Metaphor & Design Thesis
The logo for **Benyamin Namtalashvili** represents the convergence of three creative pillars:
1. **Precision Graphic Craft**: Classical typography and spatial harmony (Bauhaus / Swiss Style).
2. **Interactive Web Engineering**: Fluid motion, mathematical spring physics, and Apple VisionOS glassmorphism.
3. **Autonomous AI Automation**: Kinetic nodes, algorithmic pipelines, and high-performance execution.

---

## 2. Geometric Anatomy & Visual Structure
- **Silhouette**: Apple iOS / VisionOS Continuous Squircle (`border-radius: ~27%`, `Chamfer / Superellipse: n=4`).
- **Core Glyph**: Geometric interlocking monogram combining **'B'** and **'N'**:
  - **Left Pillar**: Solid energetic vertical stroke grounding the foundation.
  - **Upper Loop**: Architectural white titanium arc creating positive/negative space tension.
  - **Lower Loop & Dynamic Slash**: Solar Orange ribbon sweeping diagonally into the letter 'N', representing forward kinetic velocity.
  - **Spatial Focus Node**: Radiant energy dot signifying AI agent autonomous trigger execution.
- **Surface Finish**: Deep obsidian glass chassis with multi-layer specular rim highlights and a soft warm radial backlight.
