Project Description

Autonomous Lead Triage & Intelligent CRM Sync

Designed and deployed an autonomous AI lead qualification and customer support triage pipeline using n8n and Claude 3.5 Sonnet. The system normalizes inbound consultation requests from web forms and live chat in under 20 seconds, eliminating a 4.2-hour human review backlog.

The engine analyzes customer requirements, scores commercial intent from 0 to 100, dynamically schedules high-priority discovery meetings via Calendly, and syncs rich structured contact records directly into HubSpot and Slack channels for instant sales team action.
