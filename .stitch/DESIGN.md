# Graphico Design System (Editorial Design Engineer & Creative Studio)

## Vibe & Atmosphere
Ultra-sleek, editorial, and high-end. Combines stark minimalist layouts with elegant typographic contrasts (sans-serif bold mixed with italic serif accents), liquid iridescent WebGL artwork, and high-density product bento grids. It feels like an award-winning portfolio of a top-tier Design Engineer & Creative Studio.

## Visual Tokens & Palette
- **Background:** #0D0D0D (Deep warm off-black)
- **Text Primary:** #F5F5F5 (Crisp off-white)
- **Text Muted:** #888888 (Muted silver gray)
- **Accent Glow:** Iridescent liquid fluid gradient (Cobalt Blue #0055FF, Sunset Amber #FF5500, Cyan #00E5FF)
- **Borders & Cards:** #1A1A1A container borders with subtle 1px separation lines.

## Typography System
- **Headline Font:** Large, tight-leading Sans-Serif (Inter / Helvetica Neue / Monument) paired dynamically with **Serif Italic Emphasis** (Instrument Serif / Playfair Display) for key words.
- **Body & Metadata:** Clean 14px-16px Sans-Serif with Space Mono / Inter for technical labels.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Mix bold uppercase/sentence-case headers with elegant italicized serif callouts (e.g., "Design, Code & AI Workflows. *Design* Engineer.").

## Components & Layout
- **Split Hero Visuals:** Side-by-side showcase cards below the main hero title:
  - Card 1: High-resolution device mockup (laptop/tablet) on a natural wooden studio desk.
  - Card 2: Abstract liquid fluid gradient graphic (iridescent blue/orange light mesh).
- **Curated Bento Grid:** 4-column dense thumbnail grid with sharp 4px-8px rounded corners, subtle hover scale, and clean title tags below.
- **Pill Badges:** Small rounded pills (e.g., `● Available for Q3`, `Book a call`, `About`) with subtle borders.
