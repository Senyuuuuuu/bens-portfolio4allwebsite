Project Overview

Bridging the Divide Between Compelling Visual Design & Robust Technical Execution

Operating in a remote, hybrid capacity for Brayne Digital, this role required bridging the gap between compelling visual design and robust technical execution. The primary objective was to elevate the agency's digital presence and client offerings by developing cohesive marketing assets and engineering high-performing, responsive web platforms.

Rather than passing static mockups over a wall to detached developers, I owned the entire pipeline end-to-end—translating core brand positioning into tactile visual systems, and transforming those designs into pixel-perfect, accessible, and responsive code.

Core Mandate 01

Elevate Agency Digital Authority

Modernize client-facing digital real estate with sleek typography, interactive states, and responsive layouts tailored to enterprise clients.

Core Mandate 02

End-to-End Delivery Velocity

Accelerate turnaround times by directly architecting frontend templates that match approved brand identities without back-and-forth revision friction.

Technical Execution & Deliverables

Full-Lifecycle Production: Web Engineering, Conversion Funnels & Brand Collateral

To achieve the agency's commercial goals, execution was divided across three interconnected operational pillars: responsive web development, lead-capturing landing pages, and multi-channel brand assets.

Pillar 01

Responsive Web Development

Engineered and deployed dynamic websites utilizing a versatile tech stack including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Laravel, and WordPress. Ensured rigorous cross-device compatibility, performance optimization, and seamless user experiences across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Pillar 02

Conversion-Optimized Landing Pages

Designed and built targeted landing pages focused specifically on capturing qualified leads. Implemented clear visual hierarchies, frictionless form flows, and fast-loading modules that directly boosted user engagement metrics.

Pillar 03

Omnichannel Marketing Assets

Conceptualized and designed a wide array of both digital and print marketing materials, including corporate tri-fold brochures, official stationery, and promotional merchandise, ensuring strict brand consistency across physical and digital touchpoints.

Pillar 04

Print Pre-Press & Production Standards

Prepared print deliverables calibrated with exact CMYK color profiles, bleed margins, and die-lines adhering to TESDA Visual Graphic Design NC III professional standards.

Multi-Channel Agency Marketing Campaign

Integrated Digital Marketing & Technical Services Suite

Designed multi-channel agency promotional banners highlighting Brayne Digital’s end-to-end service capabilities—spanning SEO, social media marketing, bespoke website engineering, digital advertising, graphic design, and content strategy.

The Impact

Zero Translation Loss: Seamless Concept-to-Deployment Delivery

By combining graphic design with front-end development, I was able to take projects from initial visual concept straight through to final code deployment. This streamlined the production pipeline and ensured that the original design integrity was never lost in translation during the development phase, ultimately resulting in more engaging and effective web properties for the agency.

This hybrid capability drastically cut down development iteration cycles, allowing the agency to launch client initiatives on schedule with pixel-perfect execution across every viewport.
