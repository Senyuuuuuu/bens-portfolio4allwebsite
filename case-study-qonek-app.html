Project Description

Cohesive Cross-Platform Social Discovery & Real-Time Messaging

Qonek is a next-generation social communication platform built to unify content creation, live video stories, and end-to-end encrypted messaging across all Apple device form factors. The design combines a striking signature crimson identity (`#8E1E28`) with frosted glass blur layers, haptic micro-interactions, and fluid spring physics.

The suite encompasses 20+ specialized screens including full-bleed TikTok-style Video Reels, Instagram-style 24-hour Story trays, 2-column Find Friends creator directory cards, comprehensive User Profile portfolios, and a dual-column intelligence dashboard for MacBook and iPad desktop users.
